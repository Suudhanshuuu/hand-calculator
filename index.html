How to use

1. Enter how many numbers you want to add (e.g., 3)

2. Click "Start Session" and allow camera access

3. Show your hand with fingers raised for each number

4. Hold the same fingers steady for 1 second — it auto-confirms

5. Repeat for all numbers — the sum will be calculated

Tip: Good lighting and clear hand visibility improves accuracy
